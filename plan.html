Build Plan · Brand Method

The plan, in five bands

The qibla and waktu solat foundation is built and audited. What follows is ordered by leverage: first make the new features findable, then make them fully bilingual and effortless, then let the deck and the day feed each other, then grow the library, then port. Each band clears before the next begins.

P4
The native port
Where the web version honestly ends

Three things a browser cannot honestly promise: the azan that sounds when the phone is locked, the home-screen widget, and a compass that vibrates on alignment. These are the port's reasons to exist, and the web version's job until then is to say so plainly while doing everything else so well the port has a waiting audience.
