Made for the moment, not for the shelf

Baik is for

  • Anyone who wants the right dua fast, with its source on the card
  • Students, travellers, parents, and anyone learning the words for the first time
  • Readers in English or Bahasa Melayu who want both on the same card
  • People who will not accept an advertisement under an ayah

Baik is not

  • A full Qur'an reader with tafsir. The surah cards cover the surahs read daily, not the whole mushaf
  • Recorded recitation, yet. Today a speech voice reads the Arabic, and the card says so
  • A replacement for your ustaz. Scholar review is recorded per card and reads “pending” until it is done
  • A prayer times app first. The times are there, by JAKIM zone, but the deck is the product

No dua will ever be behind a price. If Premium is not for you, it ends in one tap and nothing is lost.

Is Baik really free?
Every dua, its Arabic, transliteration, meaning and source, is free forever. Premium only unlocks tools around the duas. The religious content itself is never behind a price.
Why charge for anything in a religious app?
Because the alternative is advertisements, and we will not put a banner under an ayah. Premium pays for the app, the review and the servers. It is priced as a tool, not as access to the duas.
Can I pay once instead of monthly?
Yes. Lifetime is RM358.20, two years' worth, paid once. The first 1,000 founding members pay RM129.90 for life.
What happens when the trial ends?
A reminder arrives 2 days before. If you do nothing, the plan you chose begins; if you cancel, you drop back to Free and keep your saves, notes and table.
