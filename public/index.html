Made for the moment, not for the shelf

is for

Free forever, no ads
  • Anyone who wants the right dua fast, with its source on the card
  • Students, travellers, parents, and anyone learning the words for the first time
  • Readers in English or Bahasa Melayu who want both on the same card
  • People who will not accept an advertisement under an ayah

Baik is not

  • A full Qur'an reader with tafsir. The surah cards cover the surahs read daily, not the whole mushaf
  • Recorded recitation, yet. Today a speech voice reads the Arabic, and the card says so
  • A replacement for your ustaz. Scholar review is recorded per card and reads “pending” until it is done
  • A prayer times app first. The times are there, by JAKIM zone, but the deck is the product

The same deck, in your pocket

The web version is the deck today. The app carries it with you: every card offline, a gentle daily dua if you want one, recitation downloads for travel, and Qibla and prayer times with the Hijri date.
