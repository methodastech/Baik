Qibla and prayer times

The direction, and the hour.

Both are open in the web app today, free, with no account. What a browser can do properly, they do properly. What a browser cannot do, this page says outright.

Qibla

The great-circle bearing to the Kaaba from where you stand, in degrees from true north. A compass is a magnetometer, so metal and cars pull it off true. When it drifts, find north by the sun instead, then hold the phone up and the camera view lays the line over what the lens sees.

  • Great-circle bearing to the Kaaba, with the distance
  • Sun calibration for when the compass is unreliable
  • Camera view, the qibla drawn over the live picture

Prayer times

All 60 JAKIM e-solat zones. Official times are fetched from e-solat.gov.my and kept in your browser a week at a time, so the sheet still reads with no connection. Outside Malaysia, and any time e-solat cannot be reached, the times are calculated from the sun by bisecting the altitude curve.

  • 60 JAKIM zones, official times, cached a week at a time
  • Five calculation methods, standard and Hanafi Asr
  • Geolocation and 26 cities guess your nearest zone; your own pick always wins

The web limit

A browser cannot call you to prayer.

The web version cannot sound the azan when your phone is locked. A sleeping tab has no voice, so Baik does not promise one: no background audio, no push notification, no widget. Open the page and the times are there. Close it and it is quiet. The native app is where the azan lands, and that is the reason it is being built.

Questions

Asked and answered.

Are the duas authentic?

Yes. Qur'anic text is transcribed from the Uthmani mushaf and verified against Tanzil, never typed by hand and never generated. Every card names its exact source, and if a dua cannot carry a checkable source it does not enter the deck. Scholar review is the last gate before public release: every card records whether it has been reviewed and by whom, and today that field reads pending.

Is Baik really free?

Every dua, its Arabic, transliteration, meaning and source, is free forever. Premium only unlocks tools around the duas: audio, unlimited saves, guided journeys, offline packs. The religious content itself is never behind a price.

Do I need to download the app?

No. The web version works in any browser today, free. The mobile app is on its way; it adds offline access, notifications and audio downloads. Use the web now, take the app when it lands.

When does the app launch?

It is in development. The first 1,000 users get the founding member lifetime price of RM129.90. Open the web app and you will see the plan and progress inside.

What about my privacy?

No advertisements, no selling of personal data. Your notes, saves and reading habits stay yours. Location is only ever requested for Qibla and prayer times, and only when you ask for them.

Bahasa Melayu?

Ya, insya-Allah. The deck was born bilingual; the BM layer ships with the language toggle. English is first while the Malay translations go through review.
