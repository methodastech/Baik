Competitor Read · Brand Method

The field, and the gap in it

Every solat app treats dua as filler. Every dua app treats solat as absent. The Malaysian market has strong utilities and strong content apps, and nothing that leads with the dua and carries utility-grade waktu solat behind it. That seam is the position.

7 apps readMalaysian market firstResearched 5 Aug 2026
The read, in 60 seconds

Utilities without a soul, content without a spine.

The market splits clean. On one side sit the utilities: MySolat, iSolat, TheNoor, Waktu Solat Malaysia, all competing on the same JAKIM data with the same list layout, differentiated by ads or the absence of them. On the other side sit content apps: Muslim Pro's everything-store, QalbyApp's lifestyle layer, and a shelf of Hisnul Muslim clones with no design language at all. Nobody owns the dua as a designed object. Khayr's deck is the moat. The solat and qibla work is the price of entry it has now paid.

02
The dua shelf
The direct category, briefly

Search any store for dua and the shelf is Hisnul Muslim collections: complete, authentic, and interchangeable. List layouts, ad banners, no sources shown per card, no design language. The category has content parity and zero product craft. Khayr's card anatomy, its source keys on every card, and its refusal to type Arabic by hand are all invisible to these competitors because none of them compete on integrity or on form.

They have

Coverage

Hundreds of duas per app, every occasion, every month. Khayr holds thirty. The content pipeline in the plan is how the gap closes without losing verification.

They lack

Provenance

No card-level source keys, no review states, no byte-exact Arabic discipline. Khayr's integrity rules are a durable difference no clone can fake quickly.

Nobody has

The object

A dua as a designed card you hold, flip, spread on a table and keep. The deck is the product. Everything else in the market is a list.

03
Position
One sentence, then the proof burden

The right dua for what you are facing, with the day's prayers built in. The deck answers a moment, the times anchor a day, and the two together give Khayr a daily-open reason no dua shelf has and a soul no utility has. The proof burden is threefold: surface the times so they are glanceable, grow the library through the verified pipeline, and keep the premium honest while competitors sell vapour.
